Schedule and encryption

Encryption

Backups contain password hashes and the logins of your mail and media servers. With a passphrase they are encrypted (age, AES/ChaCha20 with scrypt) before they leave this server. Keep the passphrase safe: without it a backup cannot be restored.

{{if $c.Passphrase}}{{end}} Changing the passphrase starts with a full backup. To restore older backups, enter the passphrase they were made with.
